This project brings together formal proofs of competitive bounds for **Splay,
Greedy, Cha-Cha, and classical MultiSplay**, using a shared framework for binary
search trees and their optimal offline cost.

[Explore the proof graph](proof-atlas.html){: .btn .btn--primary}
[Read the results](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality#readme){: .btn}

Dynamic optimality asks whether an online binary search tree can serve every
request sequence within a constant factor of the best algorithm that knows the
whole sequence in advance. The bounds formalized here still depend on the number
of keys. The [repository overview](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality#what-is-proved)
states the precise bounds, cost models, and scope of each result; the
[public Lean statements](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality/blob/main/DynamicOptimalityResults.lean)
are the formal entry point.

## Exploring the proofs

The [interactive proof graph](proof-atlas.html) shows the repository's Lean
declarations and their direct references. A declaration includes a theorem,
definition, type, or a declaration generated by Lean. An arrow points from a
declaration to another declaration that occurs in its type or its proof or
definition body.

Start with the module overview, or search for a declaration to inspect its
dependencies and the results that use it. You can filter by algorithm, declaration
kind, and reference kind, or switch to the full declaration graph. Click a node
to open its source and immediate neighbors. The spring layout is a visual aid;
distances do not measure proof complexity.

The graph contains repository declarations, including generated declarations.
Imported library declarations are outside its scope. The page records the exact
source revision used for the export. It is a standalone file, so you can also
[download it](proof-atlas.html){: download="dynamic-optimality-proof-atlas.html"}
and explore it offline.

## Papers and source

The principal source manuscripts are:

- Petr Chmel, Bernhard Haeupler, Richard Hladík, Michal Koucký, Antti Roeyskoe,
  Václav Rozhoň, Ondřej Sladký, and Robert E. Tarjan.
  [*Splay trees are almost dynamically optimal*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18498),
  2026.
- Daniel Čech, Bernhard Haeupler, Antti Roeyskoe, Václav Rozhoň, Robert E. Tarjan,
  and Sorrachai Yingchareonthawornchai.
  *Dynamic Optimality to within O(log log log n)*, 2026.
  Unpublished manuscript; public version forthcoming. This is the Cha-Cha analysis.

The [repository references](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality#references)
also identify the classical algorithms and related work. Reusable citations are
available in [BibTeX](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality/blob/main/docs/references.bib).
The [formalization map](https://github.com/vaclavrozhon/dynamic-optimality/blob/main/FORMALIZATION.md)
explains how the proofs fit together, and the repository includes instructions
for rebuilding the proofs and checking their logical dependencies.
